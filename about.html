Welcome to Sayue

Sayue was created for people who think deeply and feel intensely. It is a space where poetry meets philosophy, and where systems are reimagined rather than simply inherited.

Each book is an invitation to explore and question, not to accept a conclusion. The long works ask how people might live and govern well together. The poems keep the same questions company when the day is over.

I write the way one tends a lamp: not to defeat the night, only to make a room in it.
