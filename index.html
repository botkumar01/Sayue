The author

Sayue Verity

An independent writer, poet, philosopher and systems thinker.

Each book here is an invitation to explore and question rather than to accept a conclusion. The long works ask how people might live and govern well together, and the poems keep those questions company when the day is over.

I write the way one tends a lamp: not to defeat the night, only to make a room in it.
